Bolaji is a seasoned marketing professional and business growth
accelerator with over 15 years cumulative experience. He founded Just
Passion Concepts in 2009, a creative enterprise he grew from a zero
capital base to a multimillion naira business within a year as a
student entrepreneur.

Joined Insight
Publicis in 2014 as an executive trainee where he learnt the
rudiments of Integrated Marketing Communications. He worked on a
portfolio of brands notably Peak milk where he led the 60th
Anniversary campaign execution and launched Nigeria’s first growing
up Milk, Peak 456. Others include Amstel Malta, Legend, Smile, UBA,
Vitafoam, Scanfrost and Radix Pensions.

He joined Ogilvy in
2019 as Account Manager where he led Agency/Client relationship on
award winning brands such as 5Alive, Golden Penny, Carat, Sniper,
Purit and So Klin. He played a significant role in the successful
relaunch of 5Alive in Nigeria with record EOY sales.

In 2020, he rejoined
his career alma mata, Insight Publicis, to lead the legacy account
portfolio “Seven Up Bottling Company - developed marketing
solutions for her CSD brands like Pepsi, 7Up, Mirinda, Lipton iced
tea across markets. In addition were the innovation brand launches,
2Sure and Supa Komando Energy drink. He left in 2021 as Account
Director on financial services portfolio. He’s on the board of few
companies as he continually accelerates growth for people, brands and
businesses. He’s building WhyFinite from scratch to become the most
preferred marketing technology hub in Africa by 2030.

Bolaji holds a Business Administration degree and a post graduate
degree from Lagos state university and Lagos Business School
respectively. An associate member of Advertising Regulatory Council
of Nigeria (ARCON), Fellow of National Institute of Marketing of
Nigeria (NIMN) and Publicity Secretary, Association of Professional
Bodies of Nigeria (APBN). He’s attended different trainings and
recipient of awards both locally and internationally, including being
named as one of 35 under 35 Outstanding Marketing Professionals in
Nigeria at 2022 Brandcom awards. He is the Lead mentor at Bolaji
Junaid Mentorship Programme (BJuMP), a talent development platform
he’s driving with Industry Co-mentors to groom the next M.E.T
(Marketing, Entertainment, Technology) stars.
