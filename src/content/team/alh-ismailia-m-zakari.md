Born to the family of Moses Oluyemi Ayinde and Grace Olajumoke Ayinde (Nee
Ogunlewe), Olutoyin Ayinde, a Fellow of the Nigerian Institute of
Town Planners (NITP), is the Chairman, Tee-Ay-Ay Limited, a firm of
Consulting Urban Planners based in Lagos. Toyin Ayinde has built his
career as an Urban Planner in the Private Sector for over three and
half decades. He volunteered as an adjunct lecturer at the Lagos
State University for over a decade. He represented the Nigerian
Institute of Town Planners in the Committee assignment on Lagos
Magacity Region headed by Professor Akin Mabogunje. He served the
Lagos State Government meritoriously as Honourable Commissioner,
Physical Planning and Urban Development (2011-2015) under the
Babatunde Raji Fashola Administration, and in October 2020, he
assumed the office of National President of the Nigerian Institute of
Town Planners (NITP), where he demonstrated excellent service and
leadership skills for two years. 





He
was appointed Chairman of the Tribunal of Inquiry into the Collapse
of the 21-storey Structure along Gerrard Road, Ikoyi-Lagos, Nigeria
by Mr. Babajide Sanwoolu, Governor of Lagos State in November 2021,
submitting a credible report. He was appointed Member of the Board of
Trustees of NITP at the end of his tenure as National President in
November 2022. In view of his contribution in the Construction
Industry, he was awarded the Year 2022 Best President of Professional
Bodies by the African International Housing Show and was later
appointed Member of the Advisory Board of the same Organization in
November 2022. His professional practice experience cuts across
public, private and academic sectors of the profession of Urban and
Regional Planning. He was Adviser to the Planning Committee of the
first Lagos Physical Planning International Summit held in October
2024, and was very successful. He got elected as 2ndDeputy President of Association of Professional Bodies of Nigeria
(APBN) in October 2024. In his opinion, **“Planning
should precede development as thinking should precede speaking; to do
otherwise is to expect chaos.”**He believes that Nigeria and Nigerians will either organize by
adhering to physical planning principles or agonize by working
against those principles. He is married to Rhoda Ayinde, who retired
as Permanent Secretary in Lagos State, and their marriage is blessed
with children and grandchildren.
